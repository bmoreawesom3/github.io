About

A little about me

I started out at a drafting table and never quite stopped asking how to make the work easier. Nearly two decades in AEC, doing mechanical design across firms like AECOM, RS&H, and Pond, turned into a role I care about deeply. I'm a Practice Technology Specialist at Clark Nexsen, where I build the tools that make design teams faster and less frustrated.

That pull toward the "how" eventually sent me back to school. I recently finished a B.S. in Computer Software Engineering, and now I write Revit add-ins and automation using C#, the Revit API, and Dynamo, software that takes a tedious, error-prone task and just makes it disappear. I'm convinced AI is going to reshape how all of this gets done, and I'd rather be building at that edge than watching it happen.

The building doesn't stop when I close the laptop. I'm usually putting together PCs, soldering, woodworking, or under a car. Turns out tinkering and optimizing is just how I'm wired, in software and everything else.
