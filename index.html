I build things.
Here's the work.

I'm a developer and BIM manager with 20 years in AEC and a background in mechanical design. I build Revit add-ins and practical tools for designers, from small workflow fixes to full-scale automation, solving the real bottlenecks I've hit firsthand on projects.

The same itch that drives me to automate a tedious workflow shows up everywhere else in my life. When I'm not writing code, I'm building and customizing PCs, wrenching on cars, woodworking, or running something on the 3D printer. It's the same reflex every time. It's the same reflex every time. I look at something and immediately start clocking where it falls short, whether it's a loose joint, a bottleneck in a build, or a step that could be one click instead of five. Finding those weak points and fixing them is just how my brain works.
